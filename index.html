--- layout: home author_profile: true sidebar: nav: "main" --- The UK research community faces significant challenges arising from reductions and uncertainties in science funding. These decisions affect not only individual projects, but also the people, facilities, skills, and international partnerships that underpin scientific discovery. The effects extend across the research ecosystem, from students considering careers in STEM, to early-career researchers, technical specialists, universities, national laboratories, and industry partners. This website provides a central resource for information, official communications, community initiatives, media coverage, and opportunities to contribute constructively to discussions about the future of UK science.
