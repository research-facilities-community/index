### Internal Surveys
 
query widely between schools - you might be surprised whom you find is also using the multi-disciplinary facilities for their research or innovation.

To access impact on academic institutions, here are a set of questions that could be queried internally (e.g., asking about activities in the last five years) - Dissemination to Dean of School,  Associated Dean of Reserach and Innovation, University Leadership and Policy 

**Research and Research Excellence**
1. What impact has of access to STFC facilities on your research? - single choice: important / useful / limited
2. Which STFC facilities have you used? - mulitple choice: ISIS Neutron and Muon source / Diamond Light source / Central Laser facility / Hatree Centre / Other (with text box)
3. Approximately how many beamtime/facility access have you used in the last five years? What is an average number of days of access per opportunity? - text box
4. How relevant has STFC-supported research been to high-quality publications, REF-quality outputs (including potential 4* outputs), and grant success and future funding opportunities? - text box
5. Have you been involved in any research grants, projects, or collaborations that involve STFC? If yes, can you provide the approximate funding value and the project title? - text box
6. Are you planning to use other STFC facilities in the future - if so, which ones? - text box (could be combined with question 1.2)

**Skills, Training and Student Development**
1. Have your students (UG, MSc, PhD) participated in experiments, beamtime activities, or research at STFC facilities? - text box
2. Have you or your students benefited from training programmes (such as workshops and summer schools), engagement, or outreach (e.g., the "Outreach and Public Engagement Leadership Programme" by the Ogden Trust) supported by STFC? Please provide examples where possible. - text box

**Additional Comments**
- Please provide any specific examples, case studies, success stories, or concerns that would help illustrate the importance of STFC to your research, teaching, students, and the wider University. - text box
